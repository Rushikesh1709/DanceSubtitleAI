AI-Based Automatic Subtitle Generation for Indian Classical Dance Videos

Upload a dance video. The application detects the dancer's body pose, tracks movement over time and writes time-synchronised, descriptive subtitles of what is visibly happening — which you can then edit and download.

Upload a video Processing history

How it works

  1. Upload a video (Bharatanatyam is the main use case).
  2. Choose the sampling frequency and optional analyses.
  3. Wait while pose, hands and movement are analysed (real status is shown).
  4. Watch the video with its subtitles and review the timeline.
  5. Fix any text or timing in the subtitle editor.
  6. Download .srt or .vtt files (or an embedded-subtitle video if FFmpeg is installed).

Supported analysis

  • Body pose landmarks (MediaPipe Pose) and skeleton visualisation
  • Arm, knee/leg, turning and sideways movement; speed and holds
  • Basic hand shapes (which fingers are extended) — not full mudra recognition
  • Optional facial-expression estimates (smile, raised eyebrows)
  • SRT / WebVTT export, subtitle editor, history of jobs
